A little privacy, please.

BitesBro is currently a demo café website. We’re in Bangalore, open 10am–3pm and 6pm–11pm. Our street address and contact details will be announced before launch.

What we save

With your permission, we save the details you submit: your name, email, phone number, booking or pickup preferences, order, or message. Newsletter signups save your email. Please don’t include sensitive personal information in notes.

Where it goes

Form data stays in this website’s SQLite database. No advertising trackers, payment processing, or external email delivery are connected. Technical access logs may be retained by the hosting provider.

Your bag

We save item identifiers and quantities in your browser’s local storage to keep your bag between visits. No contact information is stored in the browser. Clearing your bag or browser site data removes it.

Your choices

You can browse without submitting any forms. Contact information and a data-retention policy must be published before a live launch. Until then, please use fictional contact details when trying the demo.

A note on the good stuff.

Our demo menu includes milk, gluten, and nuts. Plant-based labels describe the listed ingredients, not an allergen-free preparation environment.

Recipes and handling practices must be confirmed before opening. If you have a food allergy, please speak with the café team before ordering once we launch. Menu artwork is illustrative.
