A little privacy, please.

BitesBro is currently a demo food-ordering website. We’re in Bangalore, open 10am–3pm and 6pm–11pm. Our street address and contact details will be announced before launch.

What we save

With your permission, we save the details you submit: your name, email, phone, delivery address when supplied, food customisations, order preferences, booking, or message. Newsletter signups save your email. Please don’t include sensitive personal information in notes.

Where it goes

Form data stays in this website’s SQLite database. No advertising trackers, payment processing, or external email delivery are connected. Technical access logs may be retained by the hosting provider.

Three-role browser demonstration

When you choose Demo Mode, fictional order tickets, sample menu edits and demo preferences are stored in a separate browser namespace and shared with other tabs on this origin. No customer contact details, delivery addresses or payment details are copied into it. Reset this fictional data in Administrator → Settings. The role selector is a demonstration, not a staff sign-in.

Your bag

We save dish identifiers, selected options and quantities in your browser’s local storage to keep your bag between visits. No contact information is stored in the browser. Clearing your bag or browser site data removes it.

Your choices

You can browse without submitting any forms. Contact information and a data-retention policy must be published before a live launch. Until then, please use fictional contact details when trying the demo.

A note on the good stuff.

Our sample recipes may contain milk, gluten, egg, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, sesame and mustard. Each dish lists its own allergens in the customisation dialog. Vegetarian, non-vegetarian and plant-based labels describe the listed ingredients, not an allergen-free preparation environment.

Recipes and handling practices must be confirmed before opening. If you have a food allergy, please speak with the café team before ordering once we launch. Stock food photographs are representative. Some dishes use a clearly labelled cuisine reference photo; actual recipes and servings may differ.
